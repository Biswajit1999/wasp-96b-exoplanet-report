Why this planet made history

WASP-96 b is a "textbook" hot Saturn: hot enough for a clear, largely cloud-free atmosphere, orbiting a bright, quiet host star, with a large predicted atmospheric signal. Those three properties made it the ideal target to demonstrate JWST's transmission-spectroscopy capability to the public, and its NIRISS SOSS spectrum — showing an unmistakable water absorption feature near 1.4 microns — was the first exoplanet result released from the telescope.

A later analysis combined VLT/FORS2, NIRISS/SOSS, and NIRSpec/G395H. Multi-code retrievals strongly detect H2O, CO2, and Na; self-consistent grids favour 2–6× stellar metallicity, while SO2 receives only moderate support (ln B = 2.69). The authors also searched both JWST datasets for wavelength-dependent differences between the morning and evening terminators.

Complete spectrum hierarchy and limb audit

The supplementary archive contains 12 transmission products rather than two: symmetric spectra at several resolutions and separate NIRISS and NIRSpec morning/evening fits. The figure keeps the broad spectrum, fitted hemisphere parameters, and wavelength-dependent limb difference distinct.

The CSVs cannot independently reproduce the paper's limb-asymmetry significance. Adding the released marginal morning/evening errors in quadrature gives a 0.78σ water-band difference; the source study's 10,000 uniform-planet light-curve simulations give 2.6σ. The limb parameters are jointly fitted, but their covariance is not released in these tables. Therefore the diagonal calculation neither validates nor overturns the injection/recovery result. The scientifically supported conclusion remains tentative—not zero, and not conclusive.

The −3.40σ diagonal NIRISS mean offset is a wavelength-independent nuisance diagnostic and is degenerate with transit mid-time; it must not be presented as atmospheric structure. NIRSpec's corresponding mean ratio is 0.04σ. The analysis removes these constant offsets before inspecting wavelength-dependent residual structure.

Data and method notes

All 12 spectrum products are copied from Zenodo 17065171 with original headers and missing edge bins preserved. The source manifest records the 132 MB archive MD5 and a cross-platform subset hash.
